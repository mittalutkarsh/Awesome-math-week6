Mean · median · mode

Rules

The plan: Draw the slot line → turn every clue into a slot fact → fill the boxes (forced first; push or split) → check on the finished line.

When you see…Do this
A mean is givenWrite the Total box: Total = mean × count.
A number is missingMissing box = Total − every box you know.
The count is unknownCount = Total ÷ mean.
Boxes are added, removed or all shiftedChange the Total first, then the count, then divide.
Averages of overlapping pairsTurn each into a piece total; add and subtract whole pieces.
A long alternating lineGroup neighbours into equal pieces.
A median is givenSort; odd count → box (n + 1) ÷ 2; even → the middle pair adds to 2 × median.
x's position is unknownOne line per gap; solve each; keep x only if it lands in its gap.

Mean = Total ÷ count. Median = middle of the sorted line. Mode = tallest stack in the tally.
